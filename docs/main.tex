For this specification we will follow EBNF form therefore $[ \cdots ]$ denotes none or once and $\{ \cdots \}$ denotes none or more.
\section{Lexical}

For this section letter will refer to any letter(upper or lower case) in the Latin alphabet and digit will refer to any Arabic numeral.

\begin{ebnf} 
    <keyword> := "function" | "return" | "let" | "while" | "if" | "else" \\
    <ident> := ( <letter> | "\_" ) , { <letter> | <digit> | "\_" } \\
    <intLit> := <digit> , { <digit> } \\
    <floatLit> := <digit> , { <digit> } , "." , <digit> , { <digit> } \\
    <boolLit> := "true" | "false" \\
    <type> := "u32" | "f32" | "bool"\\
    <punctuation> := "@" | ";" | "\{" | "\}" | "(" | ")"\\
